# October 2026

## 2026-10-07

- 3D graphs can slowly orbit on their own with `setAutoOrbit()`. The orbit is off by default, pauses while people interact and resumes after a short idle, and stops while the tab is hidden or the graph is off screen ([#113](https://github.com/domudev/graphraum/issues/113)).
- Nodes can glow: set `glow` between 0 and 1 to draw a soft halo in the node's color around the few nodes you want to stand out ([#114](https://github.com/domudev/graphraum/issues/114)).
- Live force layout can keep data-defined clusters together: pass cluster indices to `createForceSimulation()` and tune `clusterStrength`. Nodes added later join their cluster ([#115](https://github.com/domudev/graphraum/issues/115)).
